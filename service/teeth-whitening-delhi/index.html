Laser Teeth Whitening Procedure at Smile Check Dental & Aesthetic Clinic

Your teeth whitening treatment is planned around your teeth and how they respond — rather than following a fixed treatment schedule which is why patients feel Smile Check Dental & Aesthetic Clinic is the best dental clinic in Lucknow for teeth whitening.

01

Your Teeth Are Assessed

Before whitening, your teeth are checked for factors such as cavities, active gum disease and tooth porosity. Your starting shade is also recorded using a shade card, giving you and your dentist a clear reference point.

02

Your Teeth Are Cleaned & Prepared

Your teeth are cleaned with scaling and polishing before whitening. A gingival barrier is then applied to protect your gums during the treatment.

03

Whitening Treatment Begins

Whitening gel is applied to the front surfaces of your natural teeth and activated using the laser. One whitening cycle takes approximately 25 minutes, with the appointment taking around 30 minutes per cycle.

04

Your Shade Is Reassessed

Your teeth are checked against the shade card again after the cycle. For example, a starting shade of A3 may reach A2 after one cycle

05

Another Cycle, If Needed

If you'd like a lighter shade, another cycle can be considered based on how your teeth have responded. Whitening isn't continued indefinitely — treatment is limited to what can be safely and biologically achieved for your teeth.

06

Desensitising Gel Is Applied

A desensitising gel is applied after whitening to help manage sensitivity. This is done at the clinic, so you don't need to apply anything overnight afterwards.

Laser Teeth Whitening: Aftercare

The first three days after whitening are particularly important. It is advised to follow a 'white diet' which involves avoiding food that can stain your teeth like tea, coffee, haldi and other strongly coloured foods and drinks.

After the first three days, you don't need to avoid these foods completely. Limiting heavily coloured foods and drinks where possible can help you maintain your whitening results for longer.

Does Teeth Whitening Hurt or Cause Sensitivity?

Most patients don't experience significant sensitivity during laser teeth whitening. Some people may notice temporary sensitivity afterwards, particularly if their teeth are more porous or respond strongly to the whitening treatment.

If you experience sensitivity during treatment, your dentist can decide whether to stop after one cycle or reassess your teeth before continuing. The treatment is adjusted according to how your teeth respond rather than following the same number of cycles for everyone.

A desensitising gel is applied after each whitening treatment as a precaution. In the clinic's experience, when sensitivity does occur, it is generally short-lived and lasts around one to two hours.

Is Teeth Whitening Permanent?

Teeth whitening isn't permanent. How long your results last depends partly on your diet and everyday habits.

At Smile Check Dental & Aesthetic Clinic, patients generally maintain their whitening results for around 6 months to a year. This varies from person to person. Some patients return for a touch-up after around three months, while others maintain their results for longer.

Tea, coffee, smoking and strongly coloured foods can cause your teeth to stain again over time. How often you have these can affect how quickly your whitening results fade.
