Wondering if you need a smile makeover?

A smile makeover combines different cosmetic dental treatments to improve the appearance of your smile.

Depending on your goals, treatment may include:

• teeth whitening
• dental veneers
• dental crowns
• gum contouring
• minor teeth alignment

Not every patient needs all of these treatments.

Some people need only one or two procedures.

The goal is a natural-looking smile that suits your face and features.
