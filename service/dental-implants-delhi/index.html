What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. It is a small titanium screw placed inside the jawbone which is biologically healthy. Once healing occurs, a crown/tooth cap is attached on top. This restores the look and function of a natural tooth. At Smile Check Dental & Aesthetic Clinic, we offer our clients premium implants from world renowned brands like Osstem, Straumann and, ZimVie.

Dental implants have three parts:

Implant – A titanium screw placed in bone.
Abutment – The connector between implant and crown/tooth cap.
Crown/Tooth Cap – The visible, natural looking replacement tooth.

Dental implant treatment is done in planned stages, designed for safety, comfort, and long-term success.
The procedure is performed under anaesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during it, and most patients return home the same day.

In the first few days, mild swelling or soreness is normal and settles quickly with basic care.
Over the next few months, the implant integrates with your jawbone, creating a strong, stable foundation for your final tooth.

Timeline And Recovery: Dental Implant Surgery

DAY OF PROCEDURE

Implant Placement

The implant is placed into the jawbone in a controlled procedure.
You can go home the same day.

FIRST FEW DAYS POST PROCEDURE

Initial Recovery

You may notice:

• mild swelling around the gums or face
• slight soreness at the implant site
• a feeling of tightness or pressure

These symptoms are temporary and usually settle within a few days with basic medication and care.

2 - 4 MONTHS

Healing Phase

The implant gradually fuses with your jawbone.
This step is important for long-term strength and stability.

4+ MONTHS

Crown Placement & Final Result

Once healing is complete, a custom crown is placed.
You can eat, speak, and smile normally with a stable, natural-looking tooth.
