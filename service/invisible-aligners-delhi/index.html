Wondering if aligners will work for your teeth?

Wondering if aligners will work for your teeth?

Invisible aligners are clear trays designed to gradually straighten teeth. They are custom-made using digital scans of your teeth.The treatment is planned with precision such that each aligner applies gentle pressure to move teeth into better alignment.

You usually wear each aligner for about 1–2 weeks before switching to the next in the series. Over time, teeth gradually move into the desired position. Unlike traditional braces, aligners are removable. You can remove them while eating, brushing, or flossing.

We offer invisible aligner treatments from renowned brands such as Invisalign and Spark.

During consultation, we discuss different aligner systems and recommend the option most suitable for your treatment.

Aligner Treatment Timeline

WEEK 1 - 2

Start Your Treatment

You begin wearing your first set of aligners.
They may feel slightly tight at first as your teeth start adjusting.
You’ll wear them for 20–22 hours a day.

MONTH 2 - 3

Visible Changes Begin

Teeth start showing early movement.
Small gaps may close and alignment begins to improve.
You’ll switch to new aligners every 1–2 weeks.

MONTH 6+

Alignment Improves

Your bite and alignment become more balanced.
Each new aligner continues refining tooth position.
Treatment feels more routine and comfortable.

6 - 18 MONTHS

Final Results

Most treatments complete within this range.
Final adjustments are made for a stable, lasting result.
Retainers are advised to maintain your new alignment.
